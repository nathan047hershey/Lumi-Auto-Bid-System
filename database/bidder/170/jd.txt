A leader in autonomous driving since 2007, Torc has spent over a decade commercializing our solutions with experienced partners. Now a part of the Daimler family, we are focused solely on developing software for automated trucks to transform how the world moves freight. Join us and catapult your career with the company that helped pioneer autonomous technology, and the first AV software company with the vision to partner directly with a truck manufacturer. Meet the Team The Senior Software Engineer will be a technical leader on the Fleet Enablement & Insights team, responsible for architecting and building systems that span two critical pipelines for Torc's autonomous truck platform: the map validation and simulation pipelines and the annotation platform. This role owns hard engineering problems behind both, designing scalable services and data pipelines that package maps for simulation, validate mapping updates before release, and the systems required for scalable web-based annotation tooling. The ideal candidate is a seasoned backend or full-stack engineer who thrives in a fast-paced environment, sets the technical bar for others, and is energized by complex data engineering, distributed systems, and geospatial simulation challenges. This position offers the opportunity to work at the intersection of software engineering, autonomous systems, and large-scale data operations, with a direct line of sight to the data that trains and validates the AV stack. What You’ll Do Architect, build, and own backend services and data pipelines that power the map validation and simulation pipelines for the autonomous trucking platform. Provide technical leadership to the annotation platform building scalable web-based annotation tooling including cuboid and polygon editing, cross-frame interpolation and track propagation, attribute editing, and review-first (accept/reject) workflows. Design robust data models and storage architectures across relational, spatial, and large-scale object stores, ensuring data integrity, lineage, and performance from ingest through delivery. Build high-performance point cloud and image rendering with three.js/WebGL: octree/LOD-based streaming formats, predictive prefetching for smooth frame scrubbing, camera-LiDAR projection, and multi-sensor overlays across a high camera count. Leverage AWS cloud services (e.g., S3, Lambda, ECS, RDS) to build and deploy scalable, reliable pipelines and services, and set the standards for how the team builds on them. Drive technical design and architecture decisions across the map validation and annotation platform domains, weighing tradeoffs, documenting direction, and aligning stakeholders. Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams including Mapping, Simulation, Annotation, and Perception to align capabilities with upstream and downstream requirements. Mentor engineers through code reviews, design reviews, and pairing, and raise the bar for engineering excellence, documentation, and operational rigor across the team. Identify and drive down technical debt, performance bottlenecks, and reliability gaps across the map validation and annotation stacks, and lead the response when they surface in production.
